# 3140913 Electrical Machine-I: Important Questions

BE Semester 4, Electrical, GTU. Five questions per chapter, most repeated first.
Selected from GTU papers Summer 2026, Winter 2025, Summer 2025, matched with the official GTU syllabus.

## Unit 1: Magnetic Fields and Magnetic Circuits

1. Compare electric circuit and magnetic circuit.
2. Explain the B-H curve of a magnetic material and its significance. What is hysteresis loss?
3. Define leakage flux, magnetic permeability, reluctance and magnetomotive force (MMF) in a magnetic circuit.
4. Derive the expression for energy stored in a magnetic field.
5. Numerical: for a magnetic circuit with an air gap, calculate reluctance, flux and the ampere-turns required. Also state Biot-Savart's law.

## Unit 2: Principles of Electromechanical Energy Conversion

1. Explain the principle of energy conservation in electromechanical energy conversion.
2. Explain a singly excited magnetic field system in detail.
3. Compare singly excited and doubly excited magnetic field systems.
4. Derive the expression for force or torque in a singly excited system using the concept of energy and co-energy.
5. Draw and explain the energy flow diagram of an electromechanical energy conversion device for motoring and generating action.

## Unit 3: DC Machines

1. Derive the EMF equation of a DC machine. Numerical: a shunt generator delivers a given load current at terminal voltage, find EMF generated, copper losses and commercial, mechanical and electrical efficiencies.
2. Derive the torque equation of a DC motor from first principles.
3. Explain the methods of speed control of a DC shunt motor (flux control, armature voltage control and armature resistance control).
4. Draw and explain the three-point starter for a DC motor. Why is a starter necessary for a DC motor?
5. Explain Swinburne's test for a DC shunt machine with diagram and equations, and state its advantages and disadvantages.

## Unit 4: Transformers

1. Derive the EMF equation of a transformer with usual notation.
2. Explain the open circuit and short circuit tests on a single-phase transformer. How are the equivalent circuit parameters and voltage regulation obtained from these tests? Numerical on voltage regulation at given load and power factor.
3. Derive the condition for maximum efficiency of a transformer. Numerical: find efficiency at full load and the load at which maximum efficiency occurs, from given iron loss and copper loss.
4. Draw the vector diagrams and winding connections for the three-phase transformer connections Dd0, Dy11 and Yd1.
5. State and explain the conditions for parallel operation of single-phase and three-phase transformers.
