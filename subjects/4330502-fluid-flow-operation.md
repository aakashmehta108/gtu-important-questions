# 4330502 Fluid Flow Operation (FFO): Important Questions

Diploma Semester 3, Chemical Engineering, GTU. Five questions per chapter, most repeated first.
Selected from all 8 GTU papers (Winter 2022, Summer 2023, Winter 2023, Summer 2024, Winter 2024, Summer 2025, Winter 2025, Summer 2026), matched with the official GTU syllabus.

## Unit I: Fluid Statics and its Applications

1. Explain the principle, construction and working of a simple U-tube manometer and derive its equation of pressure difference.
   - *Asked in 7 papers: W2022, S2023, W2023, S2024, W2024, S2025, S2026*
2. Define: Pressure, Absolute Pressure, Vacuum, Specific Gravity, Dynamic Pressure and Kinematic Viscosity.
   - *Asked in 6 papers: W2022, S2023, W2023, S2024, S2025, W2025*
3. Derive (develop) the equation of pressure in a static fluid.
   - *Asked in 5 papers: S2023, S2024, W2024, S2025, W2025 (in W2025 as hydrostatic equilibrium for an incompressible fluid)*
4. Numerical: The pressure difference over a manometer is 2452 N/m2. If the manometric fluid is carbon tetrachloride (sp. gr. 1.6) and water flowing through the pipeline fills the manometer leads, what will be the manometer reading?
   - *Asked in 3 papers: W2022, W2025, S2026*
5. Explain the differential pressure cell (its working).
   - *Asked in 3 papers: S2023, S2024, S2025*

## Unit II: Fluid Flow Phenomena

   - *Asked in 8 papers: W2022, S2023, W2023, S2024, W2024, S2025, W2025, S2026*
2. Describe the boundary layer and its formation in straight tubes, and explain boundary layer separation and wake formation.
   - *Asked in 5 papers: S2023, W2023, S2024, W2024, S2025*

5. Explain velocity field, velocity gradient, shear stress and rate of shear.
   - *Asked in 2 papers: S2023, S2024 (velocity field in both; velocity gradient, shear stress and rate of shear in S2023)*
