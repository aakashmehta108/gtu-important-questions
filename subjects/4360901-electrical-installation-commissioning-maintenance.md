# 4360901 Electrical Installation, Commissioning and Maintenance (E.I.C. & M.): Important Questions

Diploma Semester 6, Electrical Engineering, GTU. Five questions per chapter, most repeated first.
Selected from GTU papers Summer 2024, Winter 2024, Summer 2025, Winter 2025, Summer 2026, matched with the official GTU syllabus.

## Unit 1: Installation of Electrical Equipment

1. Differentiate between the foundation for static machines and rotating machines.
2. Explain the adverse effects of misalignment of the shaft of electrical machines.
3. Write the factors to be considered while installing a transformer.
4. List the lifting equipment used for loading and unloading of heavy electrical equipment and explain any three in detail.
5. Write the standard procedure for installation of a domestic solar plant.

## Unit 2: Commissioning and Testing

1. State the properties of a good insulating oil.
2. Explain the causes of deterioration of insulating oil.
3. State the methods of drying the winding of electrical equipment and explain any one method.
4. Explain the procedure for commissioning of a 3-phase induction motor.
5. State the methods of purifying insulating oil and explain any one method (for example, the filter press method).

## Unit 3: Maintenance of Electrical Equipment

1. State the advantages of preventive maintenance.
2. Explain the factors to be considered while preparing a maintenance schedule.
3. Define breakdown maintenance and explain the activities performed during breakdown maintenance.
4. Explain the reasons of failure of electrical equipment due to poor maintenance.
5. Prepare the maintenance schedule for a transformer having capacity above 1000 kVA. (The below 1000 kVA version is also asked.)

## Unit 4: Troubleshooting of Electrical Equipment

1. List the instruments required for troubleshooting and explain the functions of any four of them.
2. Prepare the troubleshooting chart for a circuit breaker for any four faults.
3. State the external causes of faults in electrical equipment.
4. Prepare the troubleshooting chart for a solar plant.
5. Prepare the troubleshooting chart for a transformer.

## Unit 5: Electrical Accidents and Safety

1. Explain the causes of electrical accidents.
2. Give a comparison between equipment earthing and system grounding.
3. State the methods of measuring earth resistance and explain any one method with a sketch.
4. Explain 'Permit to work'.
5. Explain pipe earthing with a neat sketch. (Plate earthing is also asked.)
