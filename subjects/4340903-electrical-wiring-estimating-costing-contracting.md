# 4340903 Electrical Wiring Estimating, Costing and Contracting (E.W.E.C. & C.): Important Questions

Diploma Semester 4, Electrical Engineering, GTU. Five questions per chapter, most repeated first.
Selected from previous papers of this subject Winter 2017 and 2025, matched with the official GTU syllabus.

## Unit 1: Electrical Wiring and IE Rules

1. Compare different types of wiring systems.
2. State and explain any ten tools used for electrical wiring.
3. Draw and explain the wiring diagrams for (i) staircase wiring (one lamp controlled from two places) and (ii) godown wiring for four lamps.
4. Explain the safety accessories used in electrical wiring with their specifications.
5. Explain the IE rules for electrical wiring as per the IE Act-2003.

## Unit 2: Elements of Estimating and Concepts of Contracting

1. Define estimation. Explain the factors to be considered for scientific estimation.
2. Define the terms: Contract, Tender, Earnest Money Deposit (EMD), Security Deposit and Schedule of Rates (SOR).
3. What is a contract? Give the classification of contracts and explain each type of contract.
4. List the various purchase procedures and explain each with its merits and demerits.
5. Explain the procedure for preparation, submission and opening of a tender. Also state the steps for e-tendering.

## Unit 3: Estimating and Costing of Domestic and Industrial Wiring

1. A room of 4 m x 4 m x 3.5 m is to be provided with 2 lamps, 2 tube lights, 2 ceiling fans and 2 socket outlets (5 A) by PVC conduit wiring. Draw the room layout and wiring diagram, and estimate the quantity of material required and the cost of the installation.
2. In a workshop, a 7 HP, 3-phase, 415 V, 50 Hz slip-ring induction motor having efficiency 80% and p.f. 0.8 lagging is installed 20 m away from the main board. Draw the single line diagram and wiring diagram, make a list of the quantity of material required and estimate the cost of the wiring system.
3. Explain sub-circuits. State the IE rules for the load and the number of points allowed in one sub-circuit.
4. Compare domestic wiring with industrial wiring.
5. What is panel wiring? Draw the panel and wiring diagram for a three phase 3 HP induction motor with a DOL starter.

## Unit 4: Electrification of Multistoried Building

1. A newly constructed building has three floors and each floor has four flats having a load of light, fan and socket points. Calculate the total load and the total load current of the building.
2. Give the comparison between electrification of a residential building and a multistoried building.
3. Explain the busbar and busbar chamber with a neat sketch.
4. Explain the fire alarm system and smoke detection system used in multistoried buildings.
5. Explain the use of a D.G. set as a standby power supply in a multistoried building. Also state the requirements of approval from the electrical inspector for a high rise building.

## Unit 5: Estimation of Overhead and Underground Distribution System

1. Prepare a list of materials and estimate the cost of an overhead service connection for a single storey building having a load of 4.0 kW at 230 V, 50 Hz single phase, from a service pole 20 m away.
2. A 1.00 km long 11 kV distribution line is to be installed to cater a load of 110 kVA. Estimate the material required and the cost of the project. Take span 100 m and ACSR 6/1 x 2.59 mm line conductor. Assume additional data.
3. List and explain the IE rules applicable to a service connection.
4. Draw a neat sketch of an overhead service connection and name its parts. Also explain the types of service connections.
5. A 400 V, 3-phase, 4-wire, 50 Hz overhead distribution line is 750 m long. Take a span of 50 m. Calculate the number of poles and the different insulators required, and list the other materials required for the line.
