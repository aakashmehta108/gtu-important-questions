# 3140911 Economics for Engineers: Important Questions

BE Semester 4, Electrical, GTU. Five questions per chapter, most repeated first.
Selected from GTU papers Summer 2026, Winter 2025, Summer 2025, Winter 2024, matched with the official GTU syllabus.

## Unit 1: Economic Decision Making

1. Explain the economic decision making process in detail.
2. Define economics. Why is the study of economics useful for engineers?
3. Explain the types and nature of decision making, and discuss the problems in decision making.
4. Write the characteristics and scope of engineering economics.
5. Explain price elasticity of demand with examples. Also write a note on demand and supply equilibrium with a diagram.

## Unit 2: Engineering Costs and Estimation

1. Explain with suitable examples: fixed cost, variable cost, total cost, average cost, marginal cost, recurring cost and non-recurring cost.
2. Write short notes on sunk cost and opportunity cost with examples.
3. Describe with illustration the difference between cash costs and book costs.
4. What are the types of estimates? Explain the per-unit model and the segmenting model of estimation, with advantages and disadvantages of estimating models.
5. What is a learning curve? Explain its types and benefits.

## Unit 3: Cash Flow, Interest and Equivalence

1. What is time value of money? Explain its importance in engineering economic analysis.
2. What is cash flow? State the various categories of cash flows and explain how cash flows are calculated. Explain the cash flow diagram with reference to investment activities.
3. Explain the different types of interest on capital investment. Discuss nominal interest rate and effective interest rate.
4. What is salvage value? How is it treated in project management and in cash flow analysis?
5. Numerical: calculate the equivalent present worth or future worth of a given cash flow series at a given interest rate (equivalence calculation).

## Unit 4: Cash Flow and Rate of Return Analysis

1. Explain Rate of Return (RoR) with its formula and a detailed example. Also explain Internal Rate of Return.
2. Explain the concept of incremental rate of return and incremental analysis.
3. Explain benefit-cost (B/C) ratio analysis.
4. Explain sensitivity analysis. How does it help in project selection?
5. Explain break-even analysis with a proper diagram. What is its importance in estimating the base price of a product?

## Unit 5: Inflation and Price Change

1. Define inflation. What are the different types of inflation and its causes?
2. Write the effects of inflation on the economy. Also write the effects of deflation.
3. What are the monetary measures or tools to control inflation? Explain any one in detail.
4. What is a price index? State the two basic price indexes popular in India. Compare composite and commodity indexes, and explain cost indexes in short.
5. Discuss the use of price indexes in engineering economic analysis. How is price change handled with indexes?

## Unit 6: Present Worth Analysis

1. Write a short note on present worth analysis. Explain applying present worth techniques.
2. Explain the end-of-year convention in a business model.
3. What is the borrowed money viewpoint with respect to present worth analysis?
4. Define debt repayment. State the different methods of debt repayment and describe the procedure to prepare a debt repayment plan.
5. Differentiate between present worth analysis and future worth analysis. Explain future worth analysis and the payback period in brief.
