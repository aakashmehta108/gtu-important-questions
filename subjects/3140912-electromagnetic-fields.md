# 3140912 Electromagnetic Fields: Important Questions

BE Semester 4, Electrical, GTU. Five questions per chapter, most repeated first.
Selected from GTU papers Summer 2026, Winter 2025, Summer 2025, matched with the official GTU syllabus.

## Unit 1: Review of Vector Analysis

1. Explain dot product and cross product of two vectors. Numerical: find the angle between two given vectors A and B using (i) dot product and (ii) cross product.
2. Explain cylindrical and spherical coordinate systems and give their relationship with the Cartesian coordinate system. Write the differential volume element in spherical coordinates.
3. Define divergence and explain its physical significance and properties. Numerical: verify the divergence theorem for a given D over a closed surface.
4. Define curl and write its significance in deciding the type of a field.
5. State and explain Stokes theorem with its mathematical expression. How does it relate a line integral to a surface integral?

## Unit 2: Static Electric Fields

1. State and explain Coulomb's law and define electric field intensity E. Explain how superposition is applied to find E due to multiple point charges. Numerical: E at a point due to two or more point charges.
2. State Gauss's law, write its limitations, and use it to find D and E for symmetric charge distributions (for example concentric cylinders or a spherical region). Numerical: charge enclosed from a given D using Gauss's law.
3. Derive the expression for electric field intensity due to an infinite sheet charge. Also derive E at a point on the axis of a uniformly charged circular ring, with a numerical on the ring.
4. Define electric potential, potential difference and potential gradient. Prove that E = -grad V.
5. What is an electric dipole? Derive the expressions for V and E at a distant point from the dipole and explain how they vary with distance r.

## Unit 3: Conductors, Dielectrics and Capacitance

1. Define electric flux and electric flux density D. Give the relationship between D and E. Numerical: find volume charge density rho_v when D is given.
2. Derive the continuity equation in point form. State the point form of Ohm's law and derive the relation between current I and current density J. Numerical: find E and J in a conductor for applied DC voltage.
3. Explain the phenomenon of polarization in dielectrics. Differentiate between polarization and magnetization.
4. Explain the boundary conditions at the interface of two dielectrics.
5. Derive the expression for capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor.

## Unit 4: Poisson's and Laplace's Equations

1. Derive Poisson's equation and Laplace's equation.
2. Write Laplace's equation in Cartesian, cylindrical and spherical coordinate systems.
3. State and explain the uniqueness theorem.
4. Numerical: verify that a given potential field V satisfies Laplace's equation, or find the constant k in V for which it satisfies Laplace's equation.
5. Numerical: obtain the volume charge density rho_v at a given point from a given potential field V using Poisson's equation.

## Unit 5: Steady Magnetic Fields

1. State and explain Biot-Savart's law. Using it, obtain the expression for field intensity H at the centre of a circular loop carrying current I.
2. State and explain Ampere's circuital law in integral and differential (point) form. Use it to find H at various locations in a coaxial cable.
3. Prove that for a steady magnetic field, curl H = J.
4. Explain scalar magnetic potential and vector magnetic potential.
5. Numerical: find the magnetic flux for a given H over a specified surface, or the incremental H at a point due to a given current element.

## Unit 6: Magnetic Forces, Materials and Inductance

1. State and explain the Lorentz force equation on a charged particle.
2. Derive the expression for force on a differential current element, and the force between two differential current elements.
3. Explain the magnetic boundary conditions for two magnetic materials of permeabilities mu1 and mu2.
4. Classify magnetic materials. Differentiate between ferromagnetic, paramagnetic and diamagnetic materials.
5. Define self inductance and mutual inductance and discuss inductance in magnetic circuits.

## Unit 7: Time Varying Fields and Maxwell's Equations

1. Write Maxwell's equations in point form and integral form and state the meaning of each equation.
2. State Faraday's law. Derive its expression for time-varying fields and write its point form.
3. Explain displacement current and displacement current density. Differentiate between conduction current density and displacement current density.
4. Write Maxwell's equations for static fields and for time-varying fields. Which equations change and why?
5. Starting from Ampere's circuital law, explain the inconsistency for time-varying fields and how Maxwell modified it by adding displacement current.
